About

Nothing is given. Everything is made — and it is made through effort.

I'm a backend engineer working on lending infrastructure in Gurugram. Before Airtel I built serverless data pipelines and analytics for forex brokers at LifeByte Systems, and before that I interned in information security at KPMG India. I studied at UIET, Panjab University.

Outside work I read Seneca, Camus and Dostoevsky, play guitar, and keep a nightly journal that measures effort, not outcomes. Some of those pages end up here.
